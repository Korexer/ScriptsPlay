SCRIPTSPLAY CREATOR MARKETPLACE PLAN
Version: 18 October 2026

CONFIRMED AFTER THE FIRST PLAN
- Launch is intended for multiple countries and currencies. Each supported
  country/currency pair still needs payment and payout verification.
- ScriptsPlay does not yet have a Flutterwave merchant account. Checkout and
  creator payouts cannot be activated until account approval and the delayed
  settlement product are confirmed.
- The old private worker portal should be removed from the site. Its code and
  routes are removed in this branch. Existing production database records are
  preserved until the ScriptsPlay project can be inspected and migrated safely.
- ScriptsPlay Supabase is connected. The first marketplace schema, review
  permissions, private sample storage, and application rules were applied on
  19 October 2026. Old worker records remain stored but worker API access and
  the worker welcome email are retired.
- hello@scriptsplay.com is the chosen first review account. It must be created
  and verified before the admin role can be added.
- Two retired worker Edge Functions contained SMTP passwords in older versions.
  They are disabled; those passwords must be rotated with the mail provider.

PURPOSE
ScriptsPlay will connect buyers with people who can create video, audio, music,
spoken-word, performance, and entertainment content. Creators may sell a custom
service, sell ready-made content, or apply to a buyer's casting call or project
brief. The existing whiteboard-animation service will be replaced across the
public website. Keep the ScriptsPlay colour palette, fonts, clear style, and
fast loading time. The new product must have its own design, text, and identity.

MAIN WAYS TO BUY
1. Custom Creator Offers: a buyer orders a specified video, audio, live or
   recorded performance, music piece, or local role from a creator.
2. Ready-Made Content: a buyer previews and buys a clearly defined licence to
   use a completed video or audio file.
3. Casting Calls and Project Briefs: a buyer describes a role or project,
   receives applications, and hires a suitable creator.

FIRST CATEGORIES
Scripted advertisements and user-generated-style video; acting, skits, comedy,
and film roles; voice acting, narration, poetry, and eulogies; music, beats,
instrumentals, singing, and dance. Leave room for live streams, magic, and new
categories. Launch with enough real creators in a few categories before
expanding the full catalogue.

PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
- A buyer and creator may use the same account and switch roles.
- Search by skill and product first, then narrow by category, location,
  language, format, budget, availability, delivery time, and licence terms.
- Give new and smaller creators fair exposure. Rank on relevance, sample
  quality, response, completion, and recent activity, not follower count alone.
- Show actual samples and exact deliverables before purchase.
- Protect originals before payment and record the purchased rights.
- Use plain language for prices, fees, deadlines, revisions, disputes, and
  completion rules.

PUBLIC SITE
Homepage; category index and category pages; search and results; creator
profiles; Creator Offer detail pages; ready-made content catalogue and detail
pages; casting calls; how it works; become a creator; buyer guide; trust and
safety; fees; help; about; contact; terms; privacy; buyer and creator terms;
licence rules; refund and dispute rules; and useful, search-friendly guides.
Remove stale whiteboard-animation and transcription messaging. Preserve and
redirect old public URLs where relevant. Keep sitemap, metadata, canonical
URLs, and crawlable public pages current.

PUBLIC AND SIGNED-IN HOME
Public visitors see the core promise, search, categories, real samples, how it
works, and clear paths to buy or join. Signed-in buyers see search, saved
creators/content, recent interests, relevant offers, casting calls, messages,
notifications, and orders. Creators see an actionable dashboard for offers,
applications, current work, earnings, and availability. A compressed, muted
desktop hero video uses the user-provided asset; mobile uses the brand-colour
background without any video or poster image. Respect reduced-motion settings.

CREATOR OFFER CREATION
Use a guided, auto-saving process:
1. Choose custom service, ready-made content, or physical role.
2. Enter title, category, tags, language, location, and description.
3. Define the exact deliverable, duration/format, price, turnaround,
   revisions, and optional package tiers or extras.
4. Define personal/commercial usage, exclusivity, advertising use,
   attribution, editing rights, and ownership.
5. Specify the buyer information and files needed to start.
6. Add video/audio samples, thumbnails, and portfolio media.
7. Set capacity, availability, travel area, and whether to review requests
   before an order begins.
8. Preview and submit for moderation; publish approved offers.
Build category-specific fields without making all creators fill irrelevant
questions. Allow a creator to edit, pause, or archive their offer.

DISCOVERY AND PURCHASE
Show sample media, creator identity, verified facts, price, delivery time,
licence summary, rating, and available dates where relevant. An Offer page
explains each package, extras, requirements, FAQs, creator background,
samples, reviews, and a clear purchase or contact action. Buyers can save
items. Casting calls support location, role, budget, deadline, audition
requirements, applications, shortlists, and hiring.

ORDER AND DELIVERY FLOW
Buyer selects an offer/package or accepts a custom quote. The server computes
the final price and creates a pending order. The buyer pays. A signed and
verified Flutterwave event moves the order to paid. The buyer supplies required
information. The creator works, sends a protected preview or final delivery,
and marks the service delivered. Notify the buyer immediately. The buyer may
accept or request an allowed revision. If the buyer takes no action for three
full days after verified delivery and notice, complete the order automatically
unless a dispute, revision request, refund review, chargeback, or other hold is
open. Completion makes the creator payout eligible. Record every state change,
time, actor, amount, and provider reference. Payout workers must be idempotent
and must verify settlement and transfer outcomes. A human administrator can
pause or resolve a disputed order. Ready-made content needs a separate instant
delivery and licence flow with payment verification and protected download.

FEES AND PAYMENT MATH
For an offer price of 100 units, the buyer pays 106 units: price 100 plus a 6%
buyer fee of 6. The creator receives 80 units after ScriptsPlay's 20% creator
fee of 20. ScriptsPlay's gross fee is 26 units before Flutterwave charges,
taxes, refunds, disputes, and transfer costs. Show this calculation before
checkout and in creator earnings. Store amounts in the currency's smallest
unit and define rounding in one server-side function. Never trust totals sent
by the browser. Define who absorbs provider fees before live launch.

FLUTTERWAVE ESCROW GATE
Flutterwave's currently documented split payment sends the creator's share
on the provider settlement cycle, so an ordinary split at checkout does not
meet the requested three-day hold and release rule. An older v2 escrow guide
exists, but its availability and terms for this account must be confirmed
with Flutterwave before production. Obtain explicit confirmation of the
supported hold-and-release product, allowed countries/currencies, payout
account setup, refund/dispute process, and regulatory handling. Build and test
the order state machine and fee calculation first. Do not charge customers or
call a split flow 'escrow' until provider support is confirmed. Keep payment
secrets server-side. Verify webhook signatures and confirm status, reference,
amount, and currency directly with Flutterwave. Prevent duplicate webhooks,
completions, and payouts.

CREATOR AND BUYER AREAS
Buyer: profile, saved items, casting calls, orders, messages, purchased files,
licences, reviews, invoices, notifications, and settings.
Creator: onboarding, public profile, samples, offer creation and management,
ready-made uploads, casting applications, orders, messages, earnings, payout
status, availability, reviews, notifications, and verification.

ADMINISTRATION AND TRUST
Manage users, categories, offers, media review, creator verification,
copyright reports, prohibited content, casting calls, disputes, refunds,
payout exceptions, fee settings, and audit logs. Require rights to uploaded
work and consent for people in it. Make local-role safety, travel, scheduling,
auditions, and release terms clear. Define age limits and moderation rules.
Avoid fake testimonials, impersonation, deceptive ad claims, and unlicensed
media. Keep genuine reviews tied to completed orders.

TECHNICAL STRUCTURE
Keep the existing React, TypeScript, Vite, and Vercel application unless code
review shows a strong reason to change it. Reuse the brand tokens and
performance habits. Use Supabase Auth, Postgres, Storage, and carefully scoped
Row Level Security. Keep private order media behind signed access. Use server
functions for price calculation, checkout creation, Flutterwave verification,
webhooks, scheduled completion, and payouts. Add background media processing
for thumbnails, previews, compression, and watermarking. Use database-backed
search for the first release; add a dedicated search service only if needed.
Do not expose service-role keys or payment secrets in Vite browser variables.
Preserve existing production tables and user data until their role is known.

DATA AREAS
Accounts and profiles; creator profiles; categories and skills; Creator
Offers; packages and extras; portfolio and private media; ready-made content
and licences; casting calls and applications; orders and order requirements;
delivery and revisions; conversation messages; payment attempts; immutable
fee snapshots; payout requests and outcomes; saved items; reviews;
notifications; reports; moderation decisions; and audit history.
